Pasta Primavera

Ingredients:
- 400g spaghetti or fettuccine
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 medium zucchini, sliced into half-moons
- 1 red bell pepper, julienned
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 cup broccoli florets
- 1/2 cup frozen green peas
- 1/2 cup heavy cream (or coconut cream for dairy-free)
- 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese (or nutritional yeast for vegan)
- Salt and black pepper to taste
- Fresh basil leaves for garnish
- Red chili flakes (optional)

Instructions:
1. Cook pasta in a large pot of salted boiling water according to package directions until al dente. Reserve 1/2 cup pasta water before draining.
2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
3. Add garlic and sauté for 1 minute until fragrant.
4. Add broccoli and bell pepper, cook for 3 minutes.
5. Add zucchini and cook for another 2 minutes.
6. Add cherry tomatoes and peas, cook for 2 minutes.
7. Pour in the cream and stir to combine. Let simmer for 2 minutes.
8. Add drained pasta to the pan, tossing to coat. Use reserved pasta water to loosen if needed.
9. Season with salt, pepper, and chili flakes if using.
10. Serve topped with Parmesan and fresh basil.

Nutritional Info (per serving, serves 4):
- Calories: ~520 kcal
- Protein: 16g
- Carbohydrates: 72g
- Fat: 18g
- Fibre: 6g

Variations:
- Add grilled chicken or shrimp for extra protein.
- Use gluten-free pasta for a gluten-free version.
- Replace cream with pasta water and extra olive oil for a lighter dish.

Chicken Tikka Masala

Ingredients:
Marinade:
- 500g boneless chicken thighs, cut into chunks
- 1 cup plain yogurt
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 2 teaspoons ground cumin
- 2 teaspoons ground coriander
- 1 teaspoon turmeric
- 1 teaspoon garam masala
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1-inch piece ginger, grated

Sauce:
- 2 tablespoons butter or ghee
- 1 large onion, finely diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1-inch piece ginger, grated
- 1 can (400g) crushed tomatoes
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1 teaspoon coriander
- 1 teaspoon garam masala
- 1/2 teaspoon turmeric
- 1/2 teaspoon chili powder
- 1 cup heavy cream or full-fat coconut milk
- Salt to taste
- Fresh coriander for garnish

Instructions:
1. Mix all marinade ingredients with chicken. Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours (overnight preferred).
2. Preheat grill or broiler to high. Thread chicken onto skewers and grill 10-12 minutes, turning once, until charred at edges. Set aside.
3. Melt butter in a large saucepan over medium heat. Add onion and cook 8-10 minutes until golden.
4. Add garlic and ginger, cook 2 minutes.
5. Add all spices and cook 1 minute until fragrant.
6. Add crushed tomatoes. Simmer 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
7. Use an immersion blender to puree sauce until smooth.
8. Stir in cream and add grilled chicken. Simmer 10 minutes.
9. Adjust seasoning and garnish with coriander. Serve with basmati rice or naan.

Nutritional Info (per serving, serves 4):
- Calories: ~580 kcal
- Protein: 38g
- Carbohydrates: 22g
- Fat: 36g

Avocado Black Bean Tacos (Vegan)

Ingredients:
- 8 small corn tortillas
- 1 can (400g) black beans, drained and rinsed
- 2 ripe avocados
- 1 lime, juiced
- 1 cup red cabbage, shredded
- 1/2 cup fresh salsa (tomato, onion, coriander, lime)
- 2 tablespoons pickled jalapeños
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- Salt to taste
- Fresh coriander to serve

Instructions:
1. Warm black beans in a pan with cumin, paprika, and a pinch of salt for 3-4 minutes.
2. Mash avocados with lime juice and salt to make guacamole.
3. Warm tortillas in a dry skillet or directly over a gas flame.
4. Assemble tacos: spread guacamole, top with spiced beans, cabbage, salsa, and jalapeños.
5. Garnish with fresh coriander and a squeeze of lime. Serve immediately.

Nutritional Info (per 2 tacos):
- Calories: ~380 kcal
- Protein: 12g
- Carbohydrates: 48g
- Fat: 16g
- Fibre: 14g

Substitutions:
- Use lettuce leaves instead of tortillas for low-carb.
- Add grilled corn kernels for sweetness.
- Replace jalapeños with mild pickled peppers if avoiding heat.

Classic Banana Bread

Ingredients:
- 3 very ripe bananas, mashed
- 1/3 cup melted butter or coconut oil
- 3/4 cup sugar (or 1/2 cup honey for natural sweetener)
- 1 egg, beaten (or flax egg: 1 tbsp ground flaxseed + 3 tbsp water)
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- Pinch of salt
- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour (or oat flour for gluten-free)
- Optional add-ins: 1/2 cup walnuts, chocolate chips, or blueberries

Instructions:
1. Preheat oven to 175°C (350°F). Grease a 9x5-inch loaf pan.
2. Mix mashed bananas and melted butter together.
3. Stir in sugar, egg, and vanilla.
4. Mix in baking soda and salt.
5. Fold in flour until just combined. Do not overmix.
6. Fold in optional add-ins if using.
7. Pour batter into prepared loaf pan.
8. Bake 55-65 minutes until a toothpick inserted in the centre comes out clean.
9. Cool in pan for 10 minutes before transferring to a wire rack.

Tips:
- Riper bananas (with black spots) yield a sweeter, moister bread.
- Store wrapped at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze for up to 3 months.
- Add a swirl of peanut butter into the batter before baking for extra flavour.

Greek Salad with Lemon-Herb Dressing

Ingredients:
- 3 large tomatoes, cut into wedges
- 1 large cucumber, halved and sliced
- 1 red onion, thinly sliced
- 1 cup Kalamata olives
- 200g feta cheese, crumbled or sliced
- 1 green bell pepper, sliced

Dressing:
- 4 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- Salt and black pepper to taste

Instructions:
1. Combine tomatoes, cucumber, onion, olives, and bell pepper in a large bowl.
2. Whisk together all dressing ingredients.
3. Pour dressing over salad and toss gently.
4. Top with feta cheese. Do not toss after adding feta.
5. Let rest 10 minutes before serving for flavours to meld.

Nutritional Info (per serving, serves 4):
- Calories: ~290 kcal
- Protein: 8g
- Carbohydrates: 14g
- Fat: 22g

Variations:
- Add grilled chicken or chickpeas for protein.
- Use dairy-free feta for a vegan version.
- Serve with warm pita bread.
